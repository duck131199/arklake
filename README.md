# Arklake

**Stablecoin payments, made simple.**

Arklake is an account-first payment experience designed to make stablecoin payments feel simple and familiar.

Users can create invoices, receive payments, manage supported assets and prepare payments through a clean account-based experience — while the underlying payment infrastructure stays out of the way.

## What we're building

Arklake is being designed around a simple principle:

> Payment infrastructure should be powerful underneath and simple at the surface.

The current product foundation includes:

- Account-first payment experience
- Email-oriented invoicing
- Invoice tracking and payment states
- Stablecoin wallet experience
- Receive and send flows
- Asset conversion through Swap
- Responsive web application
- Public product documentation

## Built for Arc

Arklake is being developed around Arc and stablecoin-native payment infrastructure.

The product is currently in active development. Wallet, payment, authentication and blockchain integrations shown or described in the interface may still be prototypes until their corresponding production integrations are completed.

## Current status

The public product site and App Interior V1 are now in place, providing the foundation for Arklake's core payment experience.

Current work is focused on turning the approved product flows into functional payment experiences while preserving a Web2-style user experience.
